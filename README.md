Lead Funnel & Conversion Analysis Dashboard

This project focuses on analyzing marketing and sales funnel data to understand how users move through different stages of the customer journey, from visitors to leads and finally to customers. The dashboard was developed using Microsoft Power BI to help identify major drop-off points in the funnel, evaluate the performance of different marketing channels, and understand conversion behavior. The main objective of this project is to provide meaningful business insights that can help improve customer conversion rates and optimize marketing strategies.

The project uses two datasets: the Marketing Qualified Leads dataset and the Closed Deals dataset. These datasets contain information related to lead sources, customer conversion, business segments, revenue, and contact dates. During the data preparation process, missing values and duplicate records were handled, date columns were formatted properly, and relationships between the datasets were created using the mql_id field. Power Query Editor and DAX measures were used for data cleaning and analysis.

The dashboard includes several interactive visualizations such as KPI cards, funnel charts, line charts, treemaps, scatter plots, heatmap matrices, slicers, and gauge charts. KPI cards display important metrics including total leads, total customers, conversion rate, and drop-off percentage. The funnel chart clearly visualizes the movement of users through different funnel stages, helping identify where the largest customer drop-offs occur. The line chart is used to analyze lead and customer trends over time, while the treemap highlights business segments generating the highest revenue. Scatter plots and heatmaps provide deeper insights into conversion efficiency across various lead origins and months.

Through this analysis, several key insights were identified. Organic search channels generated the highest number of leads, while paid campaigns showed better conversion efficiency. A significant drop-off was observed after the lead qualification stage, indicating opportunities for improving lead nurturing and customer engagement strategies. Certain business segments were also found to contribute more revenue compared to others. Based on these findings, recommendations such as improving landing page optimization, focusing on high-converting channels, and reducing funnel leakage were suggested.

This project helped strengthen practical skills in data cleaning, business intelligence, dashboard design, data visualization, and funnel analytics using Microsoft Power BI. It also provided hands-on experience in converting raw business data into meaningful insights that support decision-making and business growth.
